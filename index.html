About the Architect

Techno-Legal Engineering Background

I am Tarun Bhatia, founder of LawXTech (lawxtech.in). I work at the precise intersection of statutory privacy law and enterprise cloud infrastructure.

The central challenge of modern data protection is that legal teams write policies in PDF documents, while engineering teams ship code in cloud pipelines. When these worlds don't speak the same language, data breaches and regulatory fines follow.

My engineering philosophy is simple: "Law defines the boundary; Cloud code enforces it." Whether configuring keyless Purview Managed Identities, scripting JIT privilege escalation in Entra PIM, or blocking real-time Copilot prompt injection, I build verifiable systems that prove compliance to regulators and auditors on demand.
