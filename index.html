About the Practitioner

Techno-Legal Background & Focus

I am Tarun Bhatia, creator of LawXTech (lawxtech.in). I work at the intersection of statutory privacy law and enterprise cloud infrastructure.

The challenge in modern organizations is that legal teams author static compliance policies in PDFs, while cloud teams ship configurations across complex environments. When these disciplines do not align, compliance gaps and data leakage occur.

My approach focuses on: "Translating selected statutory requirements into technical controls and auditable implementation evidence." By combining hands-on console implementations with AI-assisted scripting workflows to generate, validate, and execute KQL and PowerShell audits, I build verifiable configurations that demonstrate practical security safeguards under India's DPDP Act and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standards.
