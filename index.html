About the Project

Nexora Consulting: Enterprise Privacy Architecture

Cross-border consulting firms face a difficult operational reality: employees need fast cloud collaboration and generative AI tools to serve clients, yet a single misdirected email or over-permissive folder can trigger severe statutory penalties.

Nexora Consulting is a simulated cross-border enterprise operating across India and Australia, handling two high-risk categories of personal data every day:

  • 🇮🇳
    Indian Employee & Vendor PII — Governed by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), including Permanent Account Numbers (PAN), payroll identifiers, and vendor banking records.
  • 🇦🇺
    Australian Disability Healthcare Records (NDIS) — Governed by the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Australian Privacy Principles — APPs), including NDIS Participant identifiers and support coordination plans.

Rather than relying on static PDF policies or manual reminders, I engineered an end-to-end Zero-Trust privacy governance architecture — converting statutory legal obligations directly into automated cloud guardrails.

Nexora Consulting Case Study

Enterprise Privacy Architecture in 6 Acts

A complete end-to-end Zero-Trust privacy governance implementation — from identity provisioning to live DLP blocking, Copilot AI guardrails, and statutory DSAR fulfillment across two regulatory jurisdictions.

Act 1 · Screenshots 01–05

Identity Foundation & Zero-Standing-Privilege Security

Entra ID PIM Security Groups Zero Trust
▼
⚖️
Legal & Security Challenge: Under DPDP Act Section 8(5) and Australian APP 11, data fiduciaries must enforce technical safeguards against unauthorized internal access. Standing administrative rights create a critical insider-risk exposure.
01
Workforce Directory Provisioning: Provisioned 26 directory identities across Nexora's Indian and Australian teams, separating regular business analysts from dedicated privacy leadership (DPO) and break-glass recovery accounts.
02–03
Role-Based Security Groups (12 Groups): Replaced individual user permissions with 12 role-segmented Entra Security Groups — SG-NEX-All-Employees, SG-NEX-Legal-Compliance, SG-NEX-Privacy-Operations, SG-NEX-IT-Security, SG-NEX-Finance, SG-NEX-HR, and more — so governance policies scale cleanly by department.
04
Just-In-Time Privilege Elevation (Entra PIM): Enforced zero standing privileges for Nexora's Data Protection Officer. Rather than holding permanent admin rights during routine work, the DPO holds Eligible assignments for Azure Information Protection Administrator and Compliance Administrator — activated only when needed with MFA and business justification.
05
Out-of-Band Break-Glass Isolation: Isolated Global Administrator strictly as a dedicated emergency recovery account, separated from day-to-day privacy and compliance administration — zero routine usage.

Act 2 · Screenshots 06–10 + 30

Cloud Data Residency & Hierarchical Azure RBAC

Azure RBAC Data Sovereignty KQL
▼
⚖️
Legal & Security Challenge: Cross-border consulting firms must demonstrate regional data sovereignty and ensure storage administrators cannot arbitrarily read confidential HR or Finance datasets.
06–07
Central India Data Sovereignty: Deployed Nexora's entire governance infrastructure inside Central India (rg-nexora-privacy-lab-dev-001), encompassing the Microsoft Purview governance account (purview-nexora-gov-001), segregated Finance and HR storage accounts (stnexorafinance001, stnexorahrdev001), and Azure SQL (sql-nexora-dev-001).
08
Purview Governance Plane RBAC: Locked down administrative control over purview-nexora-gov-001 so security auditors can inspect governance posture without altering classification rules.
09–10
Hierarchical Least-Privilege Storage IAM: Applied two-tier RBAC separation on stnexorafinance001. At the storage-account level: read-only metadata visibility. At the finance-documents container level: SG-NEX-Finance holds Storage Blob Data Contributor for uploads, while SG-NEX-Privacy-Operations and Purview's System-Assigned Managed Identity are restricted to Storage Blob Data Reader.
30
KQL Audit — All 11 RBAC Assignments Verified: Instead of manually clicking through 5 different Azure panels, wrote a Kusto Query Language (KQL) query in Azure Resource Graph Explorer to pull every RBAC role assignment across the subscription into one audit table — proving least-privilege access in seconds.
KQL Azure Resource Graph — RBAC Subscription Audit 
authorizationresources
| where type == "microsoft.authorization/roleassignments"
| extend principalId     = tostring(properties.principalId),
         principalType   = tostring(properties.principalType),
         roleDefinitionId = tostring(properties.roleDefinitionId),
         scope            = tostring(properties.scope)
| join kind=leftouter (
    authorizationresources
    | where type == "microsoft.authorization/roledefinitions"
    | extend roleDefinitionId = id,
             roleName = tostring(properties.roleName)
) on roleDefinitionId
| project scope, principalId, principalType, roleName
| order by scope asc

Automated Data Discovery & PII Classification

Purview Data Map PII Detection Managed Identity
▼

Legal & Security Challenge: Organizations cannot protect personal data they cannot locate. Manual data-inventory spreadsheets rapidly go stale as new vendor and payroll files are uploaded.

Act 4 · Screenshots 16–22

Cryptographic Labels, Custom NDIS Regex & Live Leak Prevention

Sensitivity Labels DLP Custom Classifiers 🔴 Live Proof
▼
⚖️
Legal & Security Challenge: Built-in classifiers detect credit cards and national tax IDs, but fail to recognize sector-specific statutory identifiers like Australian NDIS Participant IDs — leaving disability records exposed to accidental external emailing.
16–17
Encrypted Sensitivity Labeling: Engineered custom label Confidential - PII and Financial at Priority 9 (above Public, General, and Confidential) enforcing cryptographic encryption restricted exclusively to Nexora employees, published to SG-NEX-All-Employees with mandatory justification for any downgrade.
18
Custom Statutory NDIS Classifier: Built a custom Sensitive Info Type matching \bNDIS-\d{3}-\d{3}-\d{3}\b within 300 characters of healthcare keywords (NDIS, Participant, Disability, Plan Manager, Support Coordination) — detecting Australian disability record identifiers that no built-in classifier covers.
19
Automated Classification Simulation: Deployed Nexora-AutoLabel-Confidential-PII across Exchange, SharePoint, and OneDrive, capturing live rule matches to automatically upgrade sensitive files to Confidential - PII and Financial.
20–22
Real-Time Exfiltration Blocking (Live Proof): Activated Nexora-PII-Financial-Leak-Prevention across Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Teams. When an analyst attempted to email synthetic Indian PAN (ZZZPX5001J), Credit Card (4111111111111111), and NDIS Participant ID (NDIS-430-881-922) to an external Gmail — Outlook blocked the transmission immediately with a policy bounce notice.
LIVE PROOF
Email containing PAN + Credit Card + NDIS Participant ID sent to external Gmail → Outlook returned "Message Blocked: Confidential Q4 Client & Employee Payout Verification" in real-time. Policy worked as designed — zero data exfiltration.

Copilot & Generative AI Governance

Copilot DLP AI Governance 🔴 Live Proof
▼
⚖️
Legal & Security Challenge: Generative AI assistants create a rapid shadow-exfiltration channel when employees paste raw personal data into AI prompts — violating purpose limitation and data minimization duties under DPDP Act Section 6 and APP 6.
23
Dedicated Copilot DLP Guardrail: Activated Nexora-Copilot-PII-Protection across Microsoft Copilot, Copilot Chat, and Copilot Cowork — blocking AI ingestion of Indian PAN, Credit Card numbers, and Confidential - PII and Financial labelled content. Paired with a formal Generative AI Acceptable Use Policy governing external LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude).
24
Live Prompt Interception Proof: Tested by pasting synthetic PAN, Credit Card, and NDIS identifiers directly into Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat — Copilot immediately refused processing with an organizational policy restriction alert, proving the guardrail works in real-time.

Statutory DSAR Fulfillment & Third-Party Redaction

eDiscovery Premium DSAR Chain-of-Custody
▼
⚖️
Legal & Security Challenge: Under India's DPDP Act 2023 (Section 11) and Australian APP 12, individuals have the right to access their personal data — but fulfilling an access request must never expose third-party personal data stored inside multi-record corporate spreadsheets.
25
Multi-Record SharePoint Repository: Provisioned private team site Nexora-Finance-Portal hosting four vendor and financial datasets (bank_reconciliation.csv, card_transactions_test_only.csv, invoice_register.csv, vendor_master.csv).
26–27
Targeted DSAR Discovery (DSAR-2026-001): Created Purview eDiscovery (Premium) case DSAR-2026-001-Vendor-and-Employee-Access-Request and queried Demo Vendor 0002 Group across SharePoint and Exchange — accurately isolating the exact 3 matching files (272,103 bytes) while excluding unrelated card logs.
28
Third-Party PII Redaction Governance: Collected all 3 matched CSVs into DSAR-2026-001-Review-Set, identified unrelated vendors' PAN numbers co-located inside vendor_master.csv, and applied custom tag Requires-Redaction — so third-party records are stripped before external release.
29
Case-Sandboxed Reviewer RBAC & Chain-of-Custody Audit: Assigned Purview Role Group Reviewer (backed by SG-NEX-Legal-Compliance) strictly to DSAR-2026-001 — sandboxing reviewers to this single case without raw export privileges — and exported a 4-file DSAR Chain-of-Custody Audit Report (Summary.csv, Locations.csv, Settings.csv, Statistics.csv) backed by live Unified Audit Logging.

Engineering Notes

PowerShell & Automation

Key automation artifacts used to hydrate the tenant, activate audit logging, and configure compliance controls programmatically.

⚡

Exchange Online PowerShell V3

Connected via Connect-ExchangeOnline (V3 module) to activate Unified Audit Logging across the tenant — a mandatory prerequisite for eDiscovery Premium chain-of-custody records and DLP policy violation logs.

Connect-ExchangeOnline -UserPrincipalName admin@nexora.onmicrosoft.com
Set-AdminAuditLogConfig -UnifiedAuditLogIngestionEnabled $true
Get-AdminAuditLogConfig | Select UnifiedAuditLogIngestionEnabled
🔍

KQL — Azure Resource Graph

Used Kusto Query Language in Azure Resource Graph Explorer to produce a single consolidated RBAC audit table across all subscription scopes — validating all 11 role assignments meet least-privilege requirements without manual portal navigation.

authorizationresources
| where type == "microsoft.authorization/roleassignments"
| extend roleName = tostring(properties.roleName)
| project scope, principalType, roleName
| order by scope asc
